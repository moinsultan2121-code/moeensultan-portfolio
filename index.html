My Take

Every metric has a story.
Find it before you scale it.

Don't just look at the number that moved. Ask why it moved — creative, audience, offer, or something upstream nobody checked yet.

Forget the campaign for a second and look at the whole path a customer actually took: click, landing page, offer, checkout, follow-up. Somewhere in there is the real bottleneck.

That's usually where the next dollar should go.

About

The person behind the campaigns.

I'm a performance marketer who likes figuring out why an account is stuck.

I've spent the last 4+ years working across paid media, analytics, CRO, and whatever else a messy ad account decides to throw at me. I've managed accounts for 50+ brands across the US, Australia, Canada and the UK, led a team of media buyers, and moved over $15M in spend without losing track of what any of it was actually for.
